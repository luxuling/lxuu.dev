I kept losing track when I worked with coding agents. A task would move in the editor, then I would not remember where it stood. The tools I looked at were larger than the problem. I did not want a system full of pieces I would never use.

Zerouch is the small version. A workspace, the projects in it, and the next piece of work. An agent can pick an issue up and put it back. That is the whole point.

It is also the first project I serve from my own home lab.
