The problem is come from my friend that working on sales department on his office, and he tell me that he don't wanna sub service that cost a lot of money just to broadcasting WhatsApp message, and want me to build it for him self.

And i have idea why i don't charge the person monthly, and from that i search local service solution for that, and i find the [https://sumopod.com](https://sumopod.com/) that have the service that i want, and the vps for hosting my code.

Of course the service is unofficial but its got the job done, and the goal was simple user can connect their WhatsApp number via qr on the website, make the blast setting for their own and send them to the list of client number.\
