# Hello World

Welcome to my blog. If you're reading this, the site is up, the database is connected, and nothing has exploded — yet.

I'm a developer, occasionally a human being. By day I work as a front-end engineer, shipping UIs, integrating APIs, and keeping the front end from falling apart. Outside of that, I build things for fun and try to write them down along the way.

I built this site by hand — the blog engine, the design, the whole thing — partly because I wanted to learn the stack, and partly because writing about what I do forces me to actually understand it.

Expect engineering notes, side projects, and the occasional lesson learned the hard way. Minimal crying, decent progress.

Thanks for stopping by. There will be more.
